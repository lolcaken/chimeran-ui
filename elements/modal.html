ChimeraUI/Overlays/Modal

Modal

Scrim plus a 330px dialog. data-cn-open="modal" on any trigger opens the first .n1-modal in the root; scrim click and Escape close it.

Dialog
!Delete profile?
This removes the saved configuration and cannot be undone.
Markup
<button class="n1-el" data-cn-open="modal">Open dialog</button>

<div class="n1-modal" hidden>
  <div class="n1-dialog" role="dialog" aria-modal="true">
    <div class="n1-dialog-title">Delete profile?</div>
    <div class="n1-dialog-body">This cannot be undone.</div>
    <div class="n1-dialog-actions">
      <button class="n1-dialog-btn" data-cn-close>Cancel</button>
      <button class="n1-dialog-btn danger" data-cn-close>Delete</button>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
